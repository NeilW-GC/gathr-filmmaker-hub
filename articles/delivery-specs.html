Delivery Specs & Uploading Your Film

Everything you need to deliver so your film page can go live on GATHR.

The short version

You need: a Vimeo link (or HLS URL), an SRT subtitle file, a high-res poster, and your film's metadata. Theatrical screenings also need a DCP. Allow 3–5 business days after delivering everything.

Delivery checklist

  • ☐
    Film file — Upload to Vimeo (Pro or Business) and share the link. Or provide an HLS streaming URL. Minimum 1080p, H.264 codec.
  • ☐
    DCP (theatrical only) — 2K or 4K, flat or scope, 24fps, 5.1 surround or stereo. Ship us a hard drive or send a secure download link.
  • ☐
    Subtitles — SRT file required for all films. Burned-in captions are not accepted. We need a separate file so viewers can toggle them on/off.
  • ☐
    Poster image — Minimum 2000px tall. JPG or PNG. This is the main image on your film page.
  • ☐
    Production stills — 3–5 high-quality stills from the film. Used on your page and in promotional materials.
  • ☐
    Banner image — Landscape, 1920×1080 pixels. Used as the hero image on your film page.
  • ☐
    Trailer — Vimeo link to your trailer.
  • ☐
    Metadata — Synopsis (short + long), runtime, year, director, cast, genre tags, and any content warnings.

How to deliver your files

Film file (Vimeo)

Upload your film to your Vimeo account (Pro or Business plan required for privacy settings). Set the video to "Hide from Vimeo" and share the link with your GATHR contact.

Don't have a Vimeo account? An HLS streaming URL also works. Ask your post-production team or distributor.

Art assets & subtitles

Email your poster, stills, banner, and SRT file to your GATHR contact. If the files are too large for email, use Google Drive, Dropbox, or WeTransfer and share the link.

DCP (theatrical only)

For theatrical screenings at movie theaters, you'll need a DCP. You can either:

• Ship a hard drive to our fulfillment partner (we'll send you the address)

• Send a secure download link (Aspera, Signiant, or similar)

If you don't have a DCP yet, we can recommend post houses. Just ask.

Timeline

Once we have all your assets, your film page typically goes live within 3–5 business days.

The #1 cause of delays: incomplete deliveries. Send everything at once if you can. If you're waiting on one asset (like a DCP), let us know — we can set up the page and add the DCP later.
