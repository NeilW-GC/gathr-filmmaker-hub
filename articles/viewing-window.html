How Long Do I Have to Watch?

Your VOD rental doesn't last forever — here's how the viewing window works.

The short version

Most rentals give you 72 hours from the moment you press play. The clock doesn't start until you actually begin watching. Check your confirmation email for the exact window.

How the viewing window works

Your rental starts when you press play

If you bought a rental on Monday but don't press play until Thursday, your 72-hour window starts on Thursday. You haven't lost any time.

Once you press play, you have the full window (usually 72 hours) to watch the film as many times as you want.

The most common windows

• 72 hours — the most common. Starts when you first press play.

• 48 hours — some films use a shorter window.

• 7 days — some films give you a full week.

Your confirmation email tells you the exact window for your rental.

Some rentals use a fixed expiration date

Instead of a play-based window, some screenings use a fixed date (e.g., "available until March 15"). This means the rental expires on that date regardless of when you start watching.

This is common for virtual screening events where everyone gets access during the same window.

Common questions

Can I pause and come back later?

Yes. Pausing does not stop the 72-hour clock, but you can come back and resume anytime within your window. Use the same email and browser to pick up where you left off.

Can I watch it more than once?

Yes. Watch as many times as you want within your rental window.

My rental expired before I could finish

Email support@gathr.com with:

• Your name

• The film title

• The email you used to purchase

We review these on a case-by-case basis and can often help.

How do I check how much time I have left?

Check the original confirmation email — it lists your rental details. If you're mid-rental, some films show the remaining time on the player page.

Rental expired or can't find your window?

Email us with your name, the film title, and your purchase email.
