Change, Reschedule, or Cancel a Screening

Need to update your event? Here's what we can do and how to request it.

The short version

Email support@gathr.com with your event name and the change you need. Most changes are processed within 2 business days. Ticket holders are notified automatically.

What you can change

📅 Change the date or time

We can reschedule your screening as long as the new date works for the venue and the film's availability window.

Already-sold tickets transfer automatically to the new date. Every ticket holder gets an email notification — you don't need to contact them individually.

📍 Change the venue

Moving to a different location? Send us the new venue name, address, and seating capacity. We'll update your event page.

Note: If you're switching from theatrical (movie theater) to non-theatrical (private venue) or vice versa, the licensing fee may change.

💰 Change the ticket price or type

Want to switch from ticketed to free admission? Change the ticket price? Add a promo code? We can update all of this.

Price changes only apply to future ticket purchases — anyone who already bought a ticket keeps their original price.

✏️ Update event details

Need to change the event description, host name, poster image, or other details on your event page? Just tell us what needs updating.

❌ Cancel a screening

If the event hasn't happened yet, we can cancel it.

• All ticket holders receive automatic full refunds

• You are not charged a licensing fee for cancelled events

• The event page is taken down

How to request a change

Email support@gathr.com with these details:

To: support@gathr.com Subject: Screening change — [Your Event Name] Event name: [name of your screening] Change needed: [describe what you want to change] New date/time: [if applicable] Any other details: [anything else that's helpful]

Changes are typically processed within 2 business days. If your screening is less than 48 hours away, mention that in your email and we'll prioritize it.

Need to make a change right now?

Email us with your event name and what you need updated.
