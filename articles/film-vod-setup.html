Setting Up a Standalone Film VOD

Offer your film as a rental on GATHR — not tied to any specific event. Set your price, choose your window, and publish.

The short version

Go to Film Management → Video on Demand → Advanced Settings, set your price and dates, upload a cover photo, and save. It publishes automatically.

A standalone Film VOD lets any viewer rent your film at any time — it's not attached to a screening event. Think of it as your film's always-on rental option. You control the price, the viewing window, and when it's available.

Before you start

Have these ready before you go into your dashboard:

• Your film's Vimeo or HLS URL — this is set separately in Film Playback Settings, not in the VOD setup itself.

• A VOD cover photo — high-res, 16:9 aspect ratio. This is what viewers see on the GATHR Movies TV app. Different from your film poster — think of it as a banner image.

• Your rental price — what you want to charge per rental.

• Start date (and optionally an end date) — when rentals open and close.

Step-by-step setup

1

Go to Film Management → Video on Demand

Log into your GATHR account. From your filmmaker dashboard, select your film, then click the Video on Demand tab. From there, click into Advanced Settings.

2

Set your rental price

Enter the price viewers will pay to rent your film. You can also enable Pay It Forward ticketing here if your film supports it — this lets viewers pay more than the asking price to support your work.

GATHR takes a 15% commission per transaction (capped at $25), plus Stripe's payment processing fee (2.9% + $0.30). Your net payout is processed on the NET 7 schedule. Learn more about payouts →

3

Set your start and end dates

Choose when your VOD opens for rental. Set an End Date if you want it to close on a specific date — or leave it open-ended if you want it available indefinitely.

4

Configure the rental window (optional)

Under video access settings, you can set:

• Rental Period — how many days a viewer has to start watching after purchase (e.g., 30 days to press play).

• Rental Duration — how many hours to finish the film once they press play (e.g., 72 hours).

• Indefinite Access — skip the time limit entirely and give viewers unlimited access.

If you leave these at defaults, GATHR uses its standard rental window.

5

Upload your VOD cover photo

Upload a high-resolution image in 16:9 aspect ratio. This is the thumbnail that appears in the GATHR Movies TV app. It's separate from your film poster — aim for a cinematic still or key art that works as a wide banner image.

6

Save — it publishes automatically

Hit save. Your Film VOD goes live on your start date with no additional steps. Viewers can find it on your film's page and through the GATHR Movies app.

Advanced options

Once your VOD is live, you can also configure:

• Pre-orders — let viewers purchase before the start date. They get a confirmation email and a notification when it's available.

• Geo-blocking — restrict or allow access by country using a whitelist or blacklist.

• Custom pre-roll video — play a short Vimeo clip before the film starts (must be a public Vimeo URL with embedding enabled).

• Hide from profile — keep the VOD unlisted but shareable via a direct link.

• Follow-up emails — automatically send a message to viewers after they finish watching.

Common questions

What's the difference between this and event VOD?

A standalone Film VOD is always-on — any viewer can rent your film at any time through your film's page. An event VOD is tied to a specific screening: you attach the recording of that event and offer it to attendees or the public for a limited window. They're set up in different places and serve different purposes.

Can I change the price after it goes live?

Yes. Go back to Film Management → Video on Demand → Advanced Settings and update the price. Changes take effect immediately. Viewers who already purchased at the old price are not affected.

Where do viewers find my VOD?

On your film's page on GATHR and through the GATHR Movies TV app. If you've hidden it from your profile, viewers can only access it via direct link.
