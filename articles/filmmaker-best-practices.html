Best Practices

Setting Up for Success

Tips from top-performing filmmakers on GATHR.

Your Film Page is Your Storefront

Your film's page on GATHR is often the first impression a potential host or viewer has. Make it count.

1. Use a compelling trailer

Your trailer is the single most important asset. Make sure it's high quality, well-paced, and clearly communicates what your film is about. Upload it to Vimeo and link it to your GATHR profile.

2. Write a strong synopsis

Keep it under 150 words. Lead with what makes your film unique, not a plot summary. Hosts need to quickly understand why their community would care.

3. Upload high-resolution stills

Hosts use your images to promote screenings. Provide at least 3-5 production stills and your official poster in the highest resolution available.

4. Set competitive pricing

Research what similar films charge on the platform. Starting with accessible pricing can help build momentum — you can always adjust later as demand grows.

5. Respond to screening requests quickly

Hosts are often planning on a timeline. Filmmakers who respond within 24-48 hours have significantly higher booking rates than those who wait a week or more.

Remember: Every screening creates word of mouth. The filmmakers who do best on GATHR treat each screening as an opportunity to build a larger audience for the next one.

Optimize for Non-Theatrical

Non-theatrical screenings at schools, churches, and community organizations often generate the most consistent revenue. Make it easy for these hosts by:

  • Creating a simple discussion guide related to your film's themes
  • Offering a Q&A session (even via video call) as part of the screening package
  • Providing promotional materials hosts can customize
