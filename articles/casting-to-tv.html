How to Watch on Your TV

Your GATHR rental plays in a web browser. Here's how to get it on the big screen.

The short version

The most reliable way is a laptop connected to your TV with an HDMI cable. Open the rental in Chrome, go full-screen, done.

Choose your method

Most reliable

HDMI cable (laptop to TV)

1

Connect your laptop to your TV with an HDMI cable

2

Open Chrome on your laptop and go to your rental link

3

Press play and click the full-screen button

That's it. The video plays on your TV through the HDMI connection.

Need an adapter? If your laptop doesn't have an HDMI port, you need a USB-C to HDMI adapter (MacBooks) or Mini DisplayPort to HDMI adapter (older laptops). These cost $10-15 at any electronics store.

Chromecast

1

Open Chrome on your laptop or Android device

2

Go to your rental link and start playing the film

3

Click the Cast icon in Chrome's toolbar (top right, looks like a screen with WiFi waves)

4

Select your Chromecast from the list

Note: Some films may not allow casting due to copy protection (DRM). If casting doesn't work, use the HDMI method instead.

AirPlay (Apple TV)

1

On your Mac or iPhone, open the rental in Safari

2

Open Control Center and tap Screen Mirroring

3

Select your Apple TV from the list

4

Go full-screen on the video for the best picture

Smart TV browser

Some smart TVs have a built-in web browser. You can navigate to your rental link and log in directly.

This works in a pinch, but playback quality varies a lot by TV model. The HDMI method is much more reliable.

What doesn't work

Screen sharing via Zoom, Teams, or Google Meet is blocked by copy protection. Casting from some mobile apps may be restricted depending on the film. Screen recording is blocked by DRM.
