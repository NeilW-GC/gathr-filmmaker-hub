Platform Basics

What is Theatrical On Demand?

Theatrical On Demand (TOD) is GATHR's flagship model. It allows audiences to request screenings of your film at their local theater. When enough tickets are sold to meet the minimum threshold, the screening is confirmed. If the threshold isn't met, ticket holders are refunded and no screening takes place — meaning there's no financial risk to you.

What is Non-Theatrical Screening?

Non-theatrical screenings happen outside of commercial movie theaters — at schools, churches, community centers, libraries, corporate offices, and other private venues. These screenings are licensed directly and typically involve a flat fee rather than per-ticket sales.

What is VOD on GATHR?

Video on Demand allows viewers to rent your film online for a set period (typically 48-72 hours). You set the rental price and the viewing window. VOD can run alongside theatrical and non-theatrical distribution — there's no exclusivity requirement.
