Tax-Exempt & Educational Pricing

Schools, non-profits, and faith-based organizations may qualify for reduced screening fees.

The short version

Many filmmakers offer reduced fees for .edu and 501(c)(3) organizations. Select your org type when requesting a screening and the rate shows automatically. Not all films offer reduced pricing — it's up to each filmmaker.

Who qualifies

🎓 Educational institutions (.edu)

Accredited schools, colleges, and universities with a .edu email address. This includes K-12 schools, community colleges, and university departments.

What you need: Register on GATHR with your .edu email address. The educational rate (if the filmmaker offers one) appears automatically when you request a screening.

🏛️ Non-profits (501(c)(3))

Registered 501(c)(3) organizations verified by EIN. This includes community organizations, arts councils, film societies, and advocacy groups.

What you need: Select "Non-Profit" as your organization type when requesting a screening. Have your EIN (Employer Identification Number) ready — our team verifies non-profit status before applying the reduced rate.

⛪ Faith-based organizations

Churches, synagogues, mosques, and other faith communities. Most qualify for the non-profit rate.

What you need: Select "Faith-Based" when requesting a screening and provide your organization name and details.

How it works in practice

Step 1: Request a screening

Go to the film's page on GATHR and click "Request a Screening." During the process, you'll be asked for your organization type.

Step 2: See your rate

If the filmmaker offers a reduced rate for your org type, you'll see it displayed during booking. If no reduced rate is available, you'll see the standard fee.

Step 3: Verification (non-profits only)

For 501(c)(3) organizations, our team may reach out to verify your EIN before confirming the reduced rate. This usually takes 1-2 business days.

Good to know

• Not all films offer reduced pricing. It's each filmmaker's choice. If a reduced rate isn't available, you'll see the standard fee during booking.

• Educational rates are for educational use. Screenings must be connected to curriculum, campus life, or educational programming — not commercial events hosted at a school venue.

• Multiple screenings? If you're planning a series or multiple events, email us — we may be able to arrange a package rate.

Questions about pricing for your organization?

Email us with the film title, your organization name, and org type.
