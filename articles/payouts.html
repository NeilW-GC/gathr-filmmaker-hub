How Payouts Work

You earn money when people book your film. Here's when and how you get paid.

The short version

GATHR pays NET 7 — your net payout is processed within 7 days after a completed screening or closed VOD rental. Payouts go through Bill.com. You'll get an email with a statement each time.

When you get paid

1

A screening happens or a VOD rental window closes

For theatrical and non-theatrical screenings, the clock starts after the event date. For VOD rentals, it starts after the viewer's rental window expires.

2

GATHR processes your net payout (within 7 days)

We reconcile gross ticket revenue, subtract the platform fee and any admin fees, and queue your net payout. This typically takes 7 days from the event date.

3

You receive payment via Bill.com

You'll get an email from Bill.com with your payout. If it's your first time, you'll need to create a Bill.com account first (see below).

Setting up your payment profile

First-time payout? You'll receive an email invite from Bill.com to set up your payment profile. You choose how you get paid:

• Bank transfer — direct deposit to your US bank account

• Check — mailed to your address on file

• International? We can arrange PayPal or Wise. Email support@gathr.com to set this up.

Understanding your payout statement

Every payout comes with a statement showing:

• The screening or VOD event name

• Gross ticket revenue collected

• GATHR platform fee (deducted)

• Your net payout amount — what actually hits your account

If the host was a 501(c)(3) or .edu institution, the licensing fee structure may differ — this is reflected automatically in your statement.

Common questions

I haven't received my payout yet

Check that it's been at least 7 days since the event. If it has, email support@gathr.com with your film name and the event date. We'll look into it.

I need a formal invoice

Email support@gathr.com with your film name, the event, and the amount in question. We'll send you a formatted invoice.

I think the amount is wrong

Check your payout statement first — it breaks down the math. If something still looks off, email support@gathr.com with the specifics and we'll reconcile it.

Still have questions?

Email us with your film name, the event, and what you need help with.
