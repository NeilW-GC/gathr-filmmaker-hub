Reaching Your Audience

Strategies to promote your film and drive screening requests on GATHR.

Build Demand Before You Launch

The most successful films on GATHR don't wait for audiences to find them — they bring their audience with them. Start building interest before your film goes live.

Leverage Your Existing Network

  • Share your GATHR film page with your email list and social media followers
  • Reach out to organizations aligned with your film's topic or theme
  • Contact film societies, community groups, and educational institutions
  • Engage with audiences who saw your film at festivals

Partner with Hosts

Hosts are the engine of GATHR screenings. The more hosts who book your film, the wider your reach. Consider reaching out to:

  • Churches, synagogues, and faith-based organizations (for faith-related films)
  • University film departments and student organizations
  • Nonprofits working on issues related to your film's subject matter
  • Local independent theaters looking for unique programming

Pro tip: Create a simple one-page screening kit (poster, synopsis, and a link to your GATHR page) that hosts can use to promote the event in their community.

Use Social Media Effectively

Social media is one of the best tools for driving awareness. Focus on platforms where your target audience spends time, and consider these approaches:

  • Share behind-the-scenes content to build personal connection
  • Post trailer clips and audience reactions from previous screenings
  • Highlight upcoming screenings with direct links to purchase tickets
  • Encourage attendees to share their experience after watching

Email Outreach Templates

We've seen that personalized email outreach to potential hosts converts at a much higher rate than social media alone. Keep your emails short, specific, and include a clear call to action — like a direct link to request a screening.
