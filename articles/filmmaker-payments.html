Understanding Payouts & Revenue

How and when you get paid for screenings and VOD sales on GATHR.

How Revenue Works on GATHR

As a filmmaker on GATHR, you earn revenue every time someone books a screening of your film or rents it on-demand. Here's a breakdown of how it all works.

Theatrical On Demand Screenings

When a host books your film for a theatrical screening, revenue is generated from ticket sales. You set a minimum screening fee, and you receive your share once the event is confirmed and completed.

Non-Theatrical Screenings

For screenings at schools, churches, community centers, and other non-theatrical venues, you set a flat licensing fee. The host pays this fee to screen your film, and you receive your portion after the event.

Video on Demand (VOD)

When your film is available for VOD rental on GATHR, you earn revenue each time someone rents it. You set the rental price and duration window.

Tip: You can adjust your screening fees and VOD pricing at any time from your Filmmaker Dashboard.

Payout Schedule

Payouts are processed on a regular schedule. Here's what to expect:

  • Payouts are typically processed within 30 days after a screening event
  • VOD earnings are batched and paid out monthly
  • You'll receive a detailed revenue report with each payout
  • Payments are sent via the method you set up in your account (ACH, wire, or check)

Tracking Your Earnings

Your Filmmaker Dashboard gives you a real-time view of your earnings, including pending payouts, completed screenings, and VOD rental history. You can export reports at any time for your records.

Questions About a Payout?

If you have questions about a specific payout or need to update your payment information, reach out to support@gathr.com and we'll help you sort it out.
