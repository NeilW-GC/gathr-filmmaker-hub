Get Your Film on GATHR

GATHR helps independent filmmakers reach audiences through Theatrical On Demand®, non-theatrical screenings, and Video on Demand. Here's what you need to know.

The 30-Second Version

If you have the rights to your film and it's ready for distribution, you can onboard it to GATHR for free. You set your own screening fees and earn revenue when people book your film. No upfront costs, no exclusivity required.

Host a Film Screening

Bring a film to your community—whether you're a theater, school, church, library, or just someone who wants to gather people around a great story.

The 30-Second Version

Browse films on GATHR, request a screening at your venue, and we'll handle the licensing. You set the ticket price (or make it free), promote to your community, and keep a share of ticket sales. Costs depend on the film and screening type.
